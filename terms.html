Terms of Service

Last updated: October 19, 2026

These terms apply to every translation and localization project that Kakehashi Localization ("we", "us") carries out for a customer ("you"). Our services are offered to businesses only.

1. Formation of contract

A contract is formed when you reply "I agree" to our written quote and we confirm receipt of the advance payment (or the full amount).

2. Fees and payment

Fees are as stated in the quote, in the currency stated in the quote, and are based on the English source word count. Exact repeated sentences are charged at 30% of the word rate. You pay 50% before work starts and the remaining 50% within 14 days of delivery. Bank charges on your side, and any taxes withheld or imposed in your country, are borne by you so that we receive the full quoted amount. Late payments bear interest at 14.6% per year from the 30th day after the due date.

3. Scope

The scope is the content, word count, languages and file formats listed in the quote. Anything beyond that scope (additional words, new files, other formats) is quoted separately and starts only after you agree.

4. Your materials

You provide the source files, any glossary or style guide, and context where needed. You confirm that you have the right to have the materials translated. Where no glossary is provided, we choose terminology, and changes to that terminology are covered by the free revision round.

5. Use of machine translation and linguists

For the "edited machine translation" service, we produce a machine translation and a native Japanese linguist edits it in full. For "human translation", a native Japanese translator translates the text. In both cases a second person checks the result. We work with independent linguists under confidentiality obligations. Any AI tools we use are configured not to use your content for training.

6. Delivery, acceptance and revisions

Please review the delivery within 7 days; if we receive no notice by then, the delivery is accepted. Errors on our side (mistranslations, omissions, deviations from the quote) are corrected free of charge at any time. One round of preference-based or specification changes is free; further rounds are charged at our per-word rate.

7. Rights

When the full fee has been paid, all rights in the delivered translation pass to you and you may use it without restriction. Until then, those rights remain with us. Our internal tools and processes remain ours.

7a. Translation memory and glossary (optional)

With your consent, given in your reply to our quote, we keep a translation memory and glossary built from your project and use them only for your future projects. This keeps your terminology consistent and lets us translate repeated text faster; sentences that exactly match your earlier projects are charged at 30% of the word rate, the same as repeated sentences within a project. You can withdraw consent at any time, and we will then delete them.

7b. Use for quality improvement (optional)

With your separate consent, given in your reply to our quote, we may use your source text and our translation, after removing names, personal information, product names and other identifying details, to improve our internal translation and quality-check tools. Customers who consent receive a 5% discount on that project. This material is never shown or delivered to other customers. Without your consent, we do not use your content for this purpose.

8. Confidentiality

We use your materials only for your project and require the same of our linguists. Files are deleted 30 days after delivery, except for material you have allowed us to keep under sections 7a and 7b. We will sign your NDA on request.

9. Non-solicitation

You agree not to engage, directly and without us, the linguists who worked on your project and whom you came to know through it.

10. Cancellation and delays

If you cancel before work starts, we refund all payments. If you cancel after work starts, we charge for the work completed so far. If we expect to miss a deadline, we will tell you as soon as we know and you may choose: (a) a new delivery date, (b) partial delivery of the finished part with the rest later, or (c) cancellation of the unfinished part with a refund for it.

11. Limitation of liability

Except in cases of our willful misconduct or gross negligence, our liability is limited to correcting the delivery free of charge or refunding the fees you paid for the project concerned, and we are not liable for lost profits or indirect damages. You are responsible for final decisions on how the translation is used, including legal, regulatory and safety-related wording.

13. First projects

For a first project with a new customer, we may deliver a PDF preview first and release the editable files after the remaining payment is received.
