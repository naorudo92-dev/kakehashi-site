Subtitles and LQA

For the places your users actually meet the Japanese: on video, and on screen inside your product.

Japanese subtitles

字幕

Trailers, tutorials, online courses and YouTube videos. Send your English captions (SRT or VTT) and get Japanese captions back on the same timing, ready to upload. Every line is checked for reading speed, and names stay the same from episode to episode.

Japanese LQA

How a project runs

The same five steps every time, so you always know what happens next.

  1. Quote

    Send your files, video or build. We work out the size and usually reply within one business day.

  2. Agree

    Reply "I agree" and pay 50% up front by card or bank transfer.

  3. Translate

    A native linguist translates, edits or tests. Context questions come to you in one batch.

  4. Check

    A second linguist checks terms, numbers, tags and anything left out. LQA issues are verified one by one.

  5. Deliver

    Files in your format with a glossary, or an issue report for LQA. The rest is due within 14 days.

Questions

If yours is not here, email us.

Can you do a test translation first?

Yes. We translate up to 300 words of your own content for a small fee, which we deduct from your first order.

Which file formats do you accept?

JSON, YAML, PO, XLIFF, CSV, XLSX, DOCX, Markdown and HTML, plus SRT and VTT for subtitles. If yours is not listed, send a sample and we will check.

How fast can you deliver?

As a guide, about 5,000 words in 5 business days for edited machine translation, and about 2,000 words in 3 business days for human translation. Rush delivery costs 30% extra.

What do you need from us for LQA?

A build or test link, any keys or test accounts, and the platforms and parts of the product to cover. We report issues in your tracker or in a spreadsheet. Fixing them is up to your team, and we re-check the fixes on request.

Do you use AI?

For edited machine translation, yes: a machine translation is the starting point and a native linguist edits every sentence. Human translation and subtitle translation are done by a person from the start. Our AI tools are set not to use your content for training.

How do I pay?

By card (Stripe) or bank transfer, in the currency of your quote: 50% before we start, 50% within 14 days of delivery.

What if I am not happy with the translation?

Tell us within 7 days of delivery. Errors on our side are fixed free of charge at any time, and one round of style changes is free.

Do you work with translation agencies?

Yes. Agencies can use us as their English to Japanese vendor. Ask us for vendor rates.

I am a translator. Can I work with you?

If you are a native Japanese linguist based in Japan and translate English into Japanese, email us with your experience, the fields you cover and the tools you use.
